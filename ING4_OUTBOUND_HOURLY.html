Hour by hour plan
How the Inbound plan is calculated
  • Inputs: one row per OpenDock appointment — time, account and pallets (default 56, the takt study average). Add rows, paste the OpenDock list, or remove a no-show with ✕. Saved for everyone, per day.
  • Standards (minutes per pallet, per person) come from the ING-4 takt study: Unload 28 min / 56 pallets = 0.50 · Receive = Verification 0.25 + Data Entry 0.25 + Labels 0.05, plus 2 min admin per PO · Putaway 28 min / 56 = 0.50. One PO = one person per role, so capacity scales with headcount.
  • Capacity per hour = people × productive minutes in that hour × efficiency ÷ minutes per pallet. Break and Lunch from the shift settings reduce the hour.
  • Bottleneck = the step with the lowest pallets per hour for the crew entered.
  • Flow per appointment: as in the study, one person unloads a whole appointment, then one receiver processes it, then one person puts it away. A step starts when the previous step of that appointment ends and a person of that role is free. That is why putaway finishes after unloading.
  • After shift end the plan keeps going (shaded OT rows) to show the overtime needed. The crew recommendation is simulated: the smallest crew increase that finishes putaway by shift end.
  • Close steps: tap Unl, Rec and Put on each appointment when that step finishes; the button stores the time for everyone. Steps go in order; tap a ✔ to undo. Once any step is closed, finish times become a forecast: closed steps stay at their real time and open work is re-planned from now. The table shows Plan (original) vs Actual per hour.
  • Account standards: an account can have its own pallets per appointment and an observed receive time per appointment (real minutes, efficiency not applied again). LMNT starts at 40 pallets and 52.5 min (midpoint of the 45–60 min reported); type 60 to plan conservatively.
  • Appointment time: a truck can start unloading at its appointment time (or at shift start if earlier), and only when an unloader is free. If a truck arrives late, update its time and the plan recalculates.
  • Needed = the smallest crew per role that still finishes by shift end with these appointment times (simulated). A negative Gap means spare people in that role at some point of the day — usable to support the bottleneck.

Hour by hour
How it's calculated
  • Daily goal = Boxes goal typed in the Control Panel per account (ing4_goal).
  • Required rate = Goal ÷ productive hours (end − start − Break − Lunch; set either to 0 min to turn it off). The same rate is used everywhere: KPI, account cards, table and charts. The break hour gets a lower (or zero) hourly target instead of spreading the break across the day. Changing start, end or break recalculates everything instantly.
  • Prev. days = lines still counted by the Control Panel (released, not yet cleared by New Day) whose time is before today. They are in the totals but not in today's hourly average.
  • Assigned = boxes of every order line assigned today, bucketed by assignment time (timestamp in the order id). Same population as the Control Panel goal bar: dock orders that are not carry-over + released orders.
  • Completed = boxes of lines marked complete, bucketed by completion time. Released lines never marked complete use release time.
  • Open = assigned − completed (work on the floor still not closed).
  • Projection = completed + actual avg/h × productive hours left. Needed rate = (goal − completed) ÷ hours left.
  • Carry-over orders (📦 PREV DAY) are excluded, as in the Control Panel; any completed today are shown as a note. Hours are the browser's local time.
  • End of Day Recap fills the recap template from the selected day (all ING-4 accounts): date, shift, orders and boxes plan vs actual, headcount, estimated labor hours and carryover by account, plus an Hourly sheet. Trailers, accuracy, errors, safety, issues and tomorrow stay as yellow cells for the supervisor. Orders shipped = unique orders released from dock. Carryover = orders still in dock (not released) at the time of export — generate it at end of shift, before New Day.
  • History days are rebuilt from the New Day snapshot, which only stores clock times (HH:MM) — accurate to the hour, but lines with no time are listed as "no time".
